# Nimbus pricing

Nimbus has three plans. Starter costs 9 EUR per month and includes one app and 1 GB of database storage. Team costs 49 EUR per month for up to 10 apps, preview environments and 20 GB of storage. Scale costs 199 EUR per month with autoscaling, 200 GB of storage and a 99.95% uptime SLA. Annual billing saves two months on every plan.
